# Gym Fix Lab Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-13

Gym Fix Lab is designed to keep your training data under your control. This policy explains what the app stores and how it is used.

## Data Stored by the App

Gym Fix Lab may store training sessions, exercise selections, custom exercises, app preferences, subscription status, and backup export files that you choose to create.

By default, this data is stored locally on your device. The app does not require a Gym Fix Lab account.

## iCloud Sync

If you enable the Pro iCloud sync option, Gym Fix Lab uses Apple's CloudKit private database for your Apple ID to sync supported app data across your devices. The iCloud container identifier is `iCloud.com.gymfixlab.app`.

Apple provides and operates iCloud. Your iCloud settings and Apple ID account controls apply to this data.

## Purchases

Gym Fix Lab Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Gym Fix Lab can read App Store transaction status to unlock Pro features, but payment details are handled by Apple.

## Tracking and Third Parties

Gym Fix Lab does not use third-party advertising tracking and does not sell personal data.

The current app does not connect to Apple Health, Apple Watch, or external fitness services.

## Backups and Sharing

If you export a backup, the exported file is created on your device and shared only through the destination you choose in the iOS share sheet. Review the destination app or service before sharing.

## Support Requests

If you contact support, any information you provide in the message, such as screenshots, diagnostic details, or your email address, will be used only to respond to your request.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.
