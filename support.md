# Gym Fix Lab Support

For help with Gym Fix Lab, open a support issue:

https://github.com/Asun7505/gym-fix_lab/issues

When reporting an issue, include:

- Your device model
- iOS version
- Gym Fix Lab app version
- What you were doing before the issue happened
- Screenshots, if they help explain the problem

For subscription issues, open the App Store subscription settings for your Apple ID. Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple.
