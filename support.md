# Gym Fix Lab Support

For help with Gym Fix Lab, open a support issue:

https://github.com/Asun7505/gymfixlab-legal/issues

When reporting an issue, include:

- Your device model
- iOS version
- Gym Fix Lab app version
- What you were doing before the issue happened
- Screenshots, if they help explain the problem

For subscription issues, open the App Store subscription settings for your Apple ID. Purchases, cancellations, and refunds are handled by Apple. You can also use Restore Purchases in the app to recover an active subscription or permanent unlock.
